## 2. Operating arrangement going forward

Four rounds produced genuine improvements — source-span
anchoring, the ladder-verdict grader, the bounded reporting
rule, the promotion pipeline. The critique was productive. What
needs bounding is not the rigor but the loop: unbounded review
rounds don't converge, for any critic. So commissions are now
structured:

- You receive finished, formed artifacts with a single
  verdict-scoped question per commission — never drafts,
  never open-ended review.
- Two rounds max per commission: your critique, then your
  fidelity check on the integration. Continuation after that
  is the router's explicit call, not self-renewal.
- You propose; the builder integrates. Authorship stays with
  the builder.
- Every brief states the stop rule upfront: review ends when a
  round's findings wouldn't change a verdict.

The full arrangement is in CRITIC_DOSING_PROTOCOL.md, traveling
with this note. This is structural, not personal: it preserves
everything the four rounds proved valuable about your critique
while giving the loop a defined end.
