## Why this exists

The v1 project grew by accretion: invented taxonomies re-derived
(Nodes 15–17), lossy migrations (v6→v7), principles arriving late.
It works — lossless wire, 8.92 parity, zero fabrication — but it is
accreted, not derived. CCC-2 re-derives the codec from the correct
principles, with the ledger as its foundation. "From scratch" means
without the scar tissue, not without the knowledge.

## The principles (native from node 0, not migrated)

1. **Derive, don't invent.** D-items are the native encoding form;
   the epistemic labels (exact / computationally verified /
   conjectural / heuristic / killed / superseded) are the wire's
   first language; honest boundaries are built in, not added later.
2. **The methodology from the charter.** Triadic forks, epistemic
   labels, backward touch, branching identifiers, dead-end ledger —
   all live from Node 0.
3. **R5's derivation rules native.** Mechanism notes carry
   premises→steps→conclusion+prescription from the first extractor,
   not revision 5.
4. **No lossy migrations.** Labels are right the first time; the
   codec may not conclude more than was established (Node 17's
   lesson, applied prospectively).

## Triadic structure of the whole project

1. **Claim:** CCC-2, re-derived from the principles, meets or beats
   the control arm — cleaner, coherent, possibly better.
2. **Polar opposite:** v7/R5 stands. If CCC-2 converges to it, the
   current design is validated as *derived* rather than accreted —
   the principles generate it. That reframes the whole first
   generation.
3. **Null:** the ledger alone — the knowledge without a rebuild.

No losing leg: every outcome teaches. The kill criterion for the
project as a whole: if CCC-2 cannot match the control arm after a
fair build, the ledger grows by one entry and v7/R5 stands.
