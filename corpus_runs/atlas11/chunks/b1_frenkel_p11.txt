5. From GLn to other reductive groups

One adds a new dimension to the Langlands Program by considering arbitrary reductive groups instead of the group GLn. This is when some of the most beautiful

and mysterious aspects of the Program are revealed, such as the appearance of the Langlands dual group.

In this section we will trace the appearance of the dual group in the classical context and then talk about its geometrization/categorification.
