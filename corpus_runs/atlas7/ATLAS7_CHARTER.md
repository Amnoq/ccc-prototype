## Triadic fork

1. **Claim:** BSD's skies show the familiar strata — dense
   within, narrow links between — with the rank↔L-value bridge
   resolving as a small set of adjudicated links (the
   Gross–Zagier / Kolyvagin results as the gold threads).
2. **Polar opposite:** the skies interweave widely — the
   analytic and algebraic sides turn out to share substantial
   objects, and the conjecture's bridge is broad, not narrow.
   The wide links mapped are the finding.
3. **Null / controls:** (a) drafters BLIND per sky. (b) POSITIVE
   CONTROLS: documented within-lineage pairs (e.g., Kolyvagin
   building on Gross–Zagier; a rank-record computation
   extending its predecessor) MUST resolve — else method
   failure, reported plainly. (c) fabrication spot-checks;
   the user's grading packet is the final leg, as always.
