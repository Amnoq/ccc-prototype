# Perplexity retrieval prompt — Riemann Hypothesis landscape

Paste the block below into Perplexity verbatim, in a fresh chat.

---

Research task: map the current state of the Riemann Hypothesis as of 2026. Structure your answer in the numbered sections below. This is a retrieval task: for every factual claim, give (a) the exact supporting sentence from the source, quoted, and (b) the complete source URL written out as plain text (https://...) immediately after the claim — not bare footnote markers, so links survive copy-paste. Distinguish established results from open problems explicitly.

1. Precise statement. State the Riemann Hypothesis exactly: what it claims about the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function. Include the definitions needed to parse the statement (critical strip, critical line).

2. The zeta function. Give the Dirichlet series definition, the Euler product, the analytic continuation, and the functional equation — each as a verifiable claim with a source.

3. Computational record. What is the current rigorous computational verification record: how many initial nontrivial zeros have been verified to lie on the critical line, up to what height, by whom, when, and by what method (e.g., interval arithmetic). Give exact numbers.

4. Proved proportions. What proportion of nontrivial zeros is proved to lie on the critical line (Selberg, Conrey, and the most recent improvements — give authors, years, and percentages)? What proportion is proved both simple and on the critical line? Give exact figures with sources.

5. Zero-density estimates. What are zero-density estimates, what did Guth and Maynard prove in 2024, and why was it the first improvement in decades? Give the result precisely with sources.

6. Main approaches. Characterize each major line of attack in a few sentences: the Hilbert–Pólya spectral conjecture, the Montgomery–Odlyzko random-matrix-theory connection, and mollifier methods. For each: originators, the core idea, and what is established versus conjectural.

7. What remains open. What exactly is unproved; what would count as a proof; what are the recognized barriers.

After the numbered sections, add a clearly separated section labeled "Analysis" for any interpretive commentary. Keep interpretation out of sections 1–7.

## Standing rules for this instrument

- Fresh chat per retrieval run (no contamination from prior threads).
- Format compliance is graded: quoted supporting sentence + full plain-text URL per claim.
- Output is sorted before entering the record: verified / corrected / not found, with epistemic labels (established theorem / computation / conjectural / heuristic).
