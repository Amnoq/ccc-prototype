# Paper scaffold — title, scope, structure, evidence

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Status:** scaffold for the router's decisions. Title and scope are
his call (standing); the evidence assembly and structure below are
the starting point.

## Title options

1. **Narrow Bridges: Mapping the Walls Between Fields of Knowledge**
   — the wall thesis as the headline; accessible.
2. **The Sieve Generates: Novel Derivations of Prime-Distribution
   Facts from a Relational Sieve** — the prime program as headline;
   the atlas as method.
3. **Walls and Cracks: An Epistemic Atlas of Hard Problems**
   — the instrument as headline; Millennium problems as exhibits.

## Scope options

- **A (narrow):** the three novel derivations (ND-1/2/3) + the
  circle sieve, for a number-theory audience. The atlas is the
  method section.
- **B (wide):** the wall thesis across all eight atlas runs —
  prime literature, RH, BSD, AI history, markets — for a general
  science/philosophy-of-science audience. The derivations are one
  exhibit.
- **C (both):** the instrument paper (B) with the prime derivations
  as the worked example. Longest; the "most valuable thing" case
  in full.

## Proposed structure (works for B or C)

1. **Introduction** — knowledge is dense within fields, nearly
   disconnected between them; breakthroughs sit on narrow bridges.
   The instrument: blind extraction, reviewed merge, adjudicated
   links, epistemic labels, dead-end ledger.
2. **Method** — the atlas pipeline (8 runs, same discipline);
   the triadic fork (claim / polar opposite / null); controls as
   the honesty check; the grading packets.
3. **Results I: the walls** — Atlas-2 (the wall resisting RH proof),
   Atlas-5 (the RH receipt: 3 analytic skies barely touch),
   Atlas-7 (BSD: the crack is the conjecture's own bridge),
   Atlas-4 (AI's four schools).
4. **Results II: the cracks** — Atlas-6 (the Weil bridge, drawn not
   walked); Atlas-8 (4 of 5 cracks verify: PNT weight-1,
   admissibility emergent, twin constant derived, independent
   sieve); Atlas-9 (interweave, not walls — the instrument reports
   against the thesis).
5. **The three novel derivations** — ND-1/2/3 in full, with honest
   labels (framing vs. generative derivation; classical
   destinations).
6. **Discussion** — what walls mean for hard problems; the map vs.
   the proof; the appreciating asset (every sky mapped enriches
   every future crossing).
7. **Honest limits** — pilot scale throughout; coordinator
   adjudication; the polar-opposite outcomes that didn't occur;
   what the instrument cannot do (originate, decide).
